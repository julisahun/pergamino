# The Reveal

The claw mark leads down into the Curdy Sewers, past three unusually large
and unusually confident rats who have been living off drips from the Wheel
for weeks without ever finding the source. Following the smell past them
leads to a warm, round burrow, and the entire Grand Wheel of Curdmoor, being
used as a pillow by a giant mole roughly the size of a cart.

His name, once anyone thinks to ask, is Gerald. He tunnelled in because the
shrine smelled incredible, dragged the whole Wheel home the way anyone would
drag home a good pillow, and has had no idea since that an entire town has
been in an uproar about it. He is not a boss fight unless the party makes it
one — he will trade the Wheel for literally any other food, mildly confused
about why nobody offered earlier.

The Wheel goes back on its pedestal in time for the festival, missing one
Gerald-sized bite that the Mayor decides not to mention in his speech.
Gerald himself is adopted as the festival's mascot within the week, on the
theory that a monster the town caught is a much better story than a monster
the town is still afraid of. He attends the following year's festival in a
small ceremonial hat and falls asleep during the anthem.
