# The Suspects

Two leads surface fast, and both are wrong in an interesting way.

**The Order of the Sacred Rind**, met at the Content Goat, believe the Wheel
is Cheddrick's literal earthly body and that eating it is scripture, not
theft. They are loud, self-righteous about it, and — this is the
disappointing part for anyone hoping for a straightforward cult bust —
completely innocent. They wanted to eat the Wheel in a big public ceremony,
not smuggle it into a sewer. A cultist who is offended at being suspected of
*subtlety* is a good tell that this lead is a dead end.

**Inspector Mildew Grey**, found already poking around the empty shrine, was
sent by the rival town of Brinemarsh with a forged writ and genuine
professional jealousy, to sabotage the festival somehow. He arrived to find
the job already done for him and has spent the day trying to look
responsible for it, clipboard held very seriously, seal made from a
bottlecap. He is guilty of fraud and nothing else.

Neither of them noticed the claw mark. Neither of them can explain the smell.
